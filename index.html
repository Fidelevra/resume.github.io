FIELD: ABOUT

From the classroom to the data pipeline

I trained as a science teacher before moving into health data — and that background still shapes how I work: clearly, methodically, and with an instinct for making complicated information easier for other people to use.

Today my work sits at the intersection of health informatics, monitoring & evaluation, and research. Day to day, that means maintaining health information systems, producing the reports that track progress toward Plateau State's 95-95-95 HIV targets, and building the small tools and processes that make routine data work faster and more reliable. Outside my primary role, I run Archive2Analysis, a consultancy that helps facilities recover and structure years of handwritten clinical records — and I build software, like an OCR tool for digitizing scanned forms, to make that recovery work scale.
